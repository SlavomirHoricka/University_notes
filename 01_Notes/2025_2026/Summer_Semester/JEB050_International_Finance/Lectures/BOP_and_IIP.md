# Balance of Payments and International Investment Position

## Definition and Structure

The **Balance of Payments (BOP)** is a systematic record of all economic transactions between residents of a country and the rest of the world over a given period. It is a **flow indicator** — it records transactions over time (quarterly, annually), not stocks.

The fundamental identity is:

$$CA + KA + FA + \Delta Reserves + E\&O = 0$$

where:
- $CA$ = Current Account
- $KA$ = Capital Account (in narrow sense: mostly transfer of non-produced non-financial assets, e.g. debt forgiveness)
- $FA$ = Financial Account (net acquisition of foreign financial assets minus net incurrence of liabilities)
- $\Delta Reserves$ = Change in reserve assets (CB intervention)
- $E\&O$ = Errors and Omissions (statistical discrepancy)

### Double-Entry Bookkeeping

Every transaction is recorded **twice** with opposite signs — a credit (positive entry) and a debit (negative entry). The total must always sum to zero.

**Example** (Czech wine exporter):
| Transaction | Credit (+) | Debit (−) |
|---|---|---|
| Czech exporter ships wine to Germany for €10,000 | CA: export of goods +€10,000 | FA: acquisition of €-denominated bank claim −€10,000 |
| Importer pays via bank transfer | FA: foreign bank deposits +€10,000 | FA: claim extinguished −€10,000 |

## Components of the Current Account

| Sub-account | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Goods** (Merchandise) | Physical exports and imports (FOB valuation) |
| **Services** | Tourism, financial services, intellectual property, transport |
| **Primary income** | Labour compensation + investment income (dividends, interest, FDI profits) |
| **Secondary income** | Unilateral transfers: remittances, EU transfers, foreign aid |

$$CA = \text{Goods balance} + \text{Services balance} + \text{Primary income} + \text{Secondary income}$$

## Financial Account

The Financial Account records changes in cross-border ownership of financial assets. Main components:

| Category | Definition | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)** | Lasting interest; control or significant influence | **≥ 10%** of voting rights |
| **Portfolio investment** | Bonds, equities (below FDI threshold) | < 10% voting rights |
| **Other investment** | Bank loans, trade credit, currency deposits | — |
| **Financial derivatives** | Options, swaps, futures | — |
| **Reserve assets** | CB holdings of gold, SDRs, foreign currencies | — |

A **positive** FA entry = net increase in liabilities to foreigners (capital inflow). A **negative** FA entry = net increase in foreign assets held by residents (capital outflow).

## International Investment Position (IIP)

The **IIP** is the **stock** counterpart of the BOP flow. It records the total value of a country's financial assets held abroad versus its liabilities to foreigners at a given point in time.

$$\text{Net IIP} = \text{Foreign Assets} - \text{Foreign Liabilities}$$

A negative Net IIP (net debtor position) is typical for emerging economies.

### Czech IIP — September 2025 (CNB data)

| | mil. CZK |
|---|---|
| Assets | 10,034,715.5 |
| Liabilities | 10,842,845.9 |
| **Net IIP** | **−808,130.4** |

**Flow–Stock relationship:** The IIP changes due to (1) BOP financial account flows, (2) valuation changes (exchange rate movements, asset price changes), and (3) other adjustments.

## Errors and Omissions

In practice, the BOP does not balance exactly due to:
- Asymmetric data sources (exports measured by customs, imports by payments systems)
- Timing differences
- Under-reporting of capital flows

The residual is captured in the **Errors and Omissions** term:

$$E\&O = -(CA + KA + FA + \Delta Reserves)$$

Large persistent $E\&O$ can indicate illicit capital flows or significant measurement problems.

## Key Identities

Under the BOP accounting identity and assuming zero $KA$ and zero $E\&O$:

$$CA = -FA - \Delta Reserves$$

A current account deficit ($CA < 0$) must be financed by either:
- Net capital inflows ($FA > 0$, i.e. foreigners acquiring claims on the domestic economy), **or**
- Drawing down reserve assets ($\Delta Reserves < 0$)

This links directly to the saving-investment framework (see [[National_Income_Accounting_Open_Economy]]).
