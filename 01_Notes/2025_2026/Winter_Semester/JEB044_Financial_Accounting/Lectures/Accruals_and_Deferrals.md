# Accruals and Deferrals

## Why Accruals and Deferrals Exist

Under **accrual accounting**, revenues and expenses are recognised in the period they are earned/incurred, regardless of when cash is exchanged. When cash timing differs from the economic event, a temporary **balance sheet account** is created to bridge the gap. These bridging accounts are accruals and deferrals.

$$\text{Income} = \text{Value generation} \quad \neq \quad \text{Cash Flow} = \text{Cash generation}$$

Over the life of a firm: $\sum NI_t = \sum CF_t$ (cumulative equivalence). Period by period, they diverge.

## The Four-Category Matrix

The key organising principle: **which came first — cash or the economic event?**

|  | **Expenses** | **Revenues** |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash first (Deferrals)** | Prepaid Expense → Asset (Dr) | Unearned Revenue → Liability (Cr) |
| **Cash later (Accruals)** | Accrued Expense → Liability (Cr) | Accrued Revenue → Asset (Dr) |

## Category 1 — Prepaid Expense (Deferred Expense)

Cash is paid **before** the economic benefit is consumed.

- Entry at payment: Dr Prepaid Expense (Asset) / Cr Cash
- Entry at consumption: Dr Expense / Cr Prepaid Expense

**Example:** Prepaying 12 months of insurance ($1,200). Each month: Dr Insurance Expense $100 / Cr Prepaid Insurance $100.

The BS stores the unconsumed future benefit. The IS recognises the expense only as benefit is consumed (matching principle).

## Category 2 — Unearned Revenue (Deferred Revenue)

Cash is received **before** the performance obligation is fulfilled.

- Entry at receipt: Dr Cash / Cr Unearned Revenue (Liability)
- Entry at fulfilment: Dr Unearned Revenue / Cr Revenue

**Example:** Annual subscription ($1,200 received upfront). Each month: Dr Unearned Revenue $100 / Cr Subscription Revenue $100.

The BS liability signals an obligation still owed to the customer. Revenue is deferred until earned.

## Category 3 — Accrued Expense (Accrued Liability)

Expense is incurred **before** cash is paid.

- Entry at period end: Dr Expense / Cr Accrued Liability
- Entry at payment: Dr Accrued Liability / Cr Cash

**Example:** Employees earn wages of $500 in December, paid in January.
- December 31: Dr Wages Expense $500 / Cr Wages Payable $500
- January payment: Dr Wages Payable $500 / Cr Cash $500

## Category 4 — Accrued Revenue (Accrued Asset)

Revenue is earned **before** cash is received.

- Entry at period end: Dr Accrued Revenue (Asset) / Cr Revenue
- Entry at collection: Dr Cash / Cr Accrued Revenue

**Example:** $200 of interest earned on a loan, not yet received.
- December 31: Dr Interest Receivable $200 / Cr Interest Revenue $200
- On collection: Dr Cash $200 / Cr Interest Receivable $200

## Corresponding Balance Sheet Accounts

Each income statement account has a corresponding BS account that buffers timing differences:

| Revenue/Expense | Preceding BS Account | Following BS Account |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Revenue | Unearned Revenue (Li) | Accounts Receivable (As) |
| COGS | Inventory (As) | Accounts Payable (Li) |
| Operating Expenses | Prepaid Expense (As) | Accrued Liability (Li) |

## Impact on the Indirect Method SCF

The indirect SCF undoes accrual adjustments by adding back balance sheet changes:

| Working Capital Change | Adjustment to NI | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| ↑ AR | Subtract | Revenue recognised > cash received |
| ↓ AR | Add | Cash received > revenue recognised |
| ↑ Inventory | Subtract | Cash paid > COGS expensed |
| ↑ Prepaid | Subtract | Cash paid > expense recognised |
| ↑ AP | Add | Expense recognised > cash paid |
| ↑ Unearned Revenue | Add | Cash received > revenue recognised |
| ↑ Accrued Liabilities | Add | Expense recognised > cash paid |

## Cash Payment Classification Framework

When recording a cash payment, the question is: what period does the benefit relate to?

| Benefit Timing | Balance Sheet Effect | Income Statement Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Future benefit** | ↑ Asset (capitalise) | None yet |
| **Current benefit** | None | Expense immediately |
| **Past benefit (payoff of payable)** | ↓ Liability | Expense was recognised earlier |
