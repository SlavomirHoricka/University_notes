# Balance Sheet Elements

## IFRS Definitions

### Asset (IFRS Conceptual Framework)

> A **resource controlled by the enterprise** as a result of past events, from which **future economic benefits** are expected to flow to the enterprise.

Three criteria must all be satisfied:
1. **Control** — the entity has the right to direct the use of the resource and obtain its benefits.
2. **Past event** — the control arose from a completed transaction or event (not a future contract).
3. **Future economic benefit** — expected inflow of cash or cash equivalent.

### Liability (IFRS Conceptual Framework)

> A **present obligation** of the enterprise arising from past events, the settlement of which is expected to result in an **outflow of resources** embodying economic benefits.

Key elements: present obligation (legal or constructive), past event, expected outflow.

### Equity (FASB / Residual Definition)

> The **residual interest** in the assets of the entity after deducting all its liabilities.

$$\text{Equity} = \text{Assets} - \text{Liabilities}$$

## Balance Sheet Structure

### Assets Side

**Current Assets** (expected to be converted to cash, sold, or consumed within one year or one operating cycle, whichever is longer — IFRS definition):

| Item | Nature |
|---|---|
| Cash and cash equivalents | Most liquid |
| Marketable securities | Short-term investments |
| Accounts receivable (AR) | Amounts owed by customers |
| Inventories | Goods held for sale |
| Prepaid expenses | Future benefits already paid |

**Non-Current Assets:**

| Item | Nature |
|---|---|
| Long-term investments | Equity stakes, bonds held >1 year |
| Property, Plant & Equipment (PP&E) | Tangible fixed assets, net of depreciation |
| Intangible assets | Patents, trademarks, goodwill |

### Liabilities Side

**Current Liabilities** (due within one year):

| Item | Nature |
|---|---|
| Accounts payable (AP) | Amounts owed to suppliers |
| Accrued expenses | Expenses incurred but not yet paid |
| Notes payable (short-term) | Short-term borrowings |
| Current maturity of LT debt | Portion of long-term debt due within 1 year |
| Unearned revenue | Cash received, performance obligation not yet fulfilled |

**Non-Current Liabilities:**

| Item | Nature |
|---|---|
| Long-term debt | Bonds payable, bank loans >1 year |
| Minority interest (non-controlling interest) | Equity claim of outside shareholders in consolidated subsidiary |

### Equity Section

| Item | Nature |
|---|---|
| Contributed capital (paid-in capital) | Amount paid by shareholders for shares |
| Retained earnings (ARE) | Cumulative net income less cumulative dividends |
| Accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI) | Unrealised gains/losses bypassing the IS |

## Asset Classification Principle

Assets are ordered by **liquidity** (ease of conversion to cash). The most liquid appear first (cash), the least liquid last (intangibles). This ordering directly serves creditors assessing short-term solvency.

## Liability Classification Principle

Liabilities are ordered by **maturity** (imminence of repayment). Current liabilities (due within one year) precede long-term liabilities.

## The Current Assets IFRS Rule

An asset is classified as current if it is:
- cash or a cash equivalent,
- held primarily for trading,
- expected to be realised within 12 months of the balance sheet date, or
- expected to be realised within the **entity's operating cycle** (even if longer than 12 months).

The operating-cycle provision means that a distillery ageing whisky for three years may still classify the inventory as current if three years is its normal operating cycle.

## Minority Interest on the Balance Sheet

When a parent company consolidates a subsidiary it does not fully own, the portion of the subsidiary's net assets belonging to outside shareholders is reported as **minority interest** (non-controlling interest) within the **equity section** of the consolidated balance sheet. See [[Minority_Interest]] for the full treatment.
