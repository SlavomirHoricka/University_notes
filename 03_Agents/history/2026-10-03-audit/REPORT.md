## Ownership and executable skills

The full [responsibility/artifact matrix](../../LEARNING_ARCHITECTURE.md#responsibility-and-artifact-matrix) defines one writer per authoritative artifact. Ingest owns notes/assets and completion/freshness; plan owns objectives, reasons, routes, explanations, keys, rubrics, note-version manifests, version history and reassessment definitions; teach owns actual observations/session state; recall owns schedules, verified Todoist mappings, synchronization journals and tasks. Recall no longer writes new scheduling events into learner logs. No task completion or missed date becomes learning evidence.

Each skill now defines triggers/exclusions, exact inputs and paths, source boundaries, ordered decisions, readiness/failure handling, safe writes, recovery, owner handoffs and required final output. Ingest's explanatory standard, source coverage, independent fact checking, source-injection boundary, unreadable-source stops, academic conflict preservation and protection of existing user text are retained. It now blocks freshness before changing finished notes and passes exact completion/revision/locator information to plan.

Plan requires small executable lesson blocks: observable/versioned objectives; prerequisite checks and dependency reasons; teaching order; motivation, definitions, notation, assumptions and limits; intermediate reasoning; worked examples; faded and independent practice; complete keys; component rubrics/alternatives/errors/tools; misconceptions with specific corrections/fresh tasks; separate delayed/discrimination/transfer checks; and explicit advance/pause/escalation rules. It maps scope and omissions without claiming syllabus/exam completeness. Teach may execute/rephrase prepared routes, but missing keys, exhausted variants or unprepared alternatives return to plan.

Stable objective/revision/criterion/source tuples accompany evidence. Previous definitions are immutable; criteria are not retroactively applied. Plan provides exact compatible-version mappings or named reassessment gates; teach supplies new observed evidence; recall consumes that decision without academic reconciliation or grading. Legacy Econometrics I records remain unchanged and are not silently promoted to the new schema.

## Methodology and scheduling

[LEARNING_METHODS.md](../../references/LEARNING_METHODS.md) contains primary/authoritative evidence, limitations, operational application and source-access provenance checked on 2026-10-03, including 2025/2026 findings. Ordinary instructions are embedded in the skill bodies: retrieval before feedback; correction with fresh checks; worked examples with fading; necessary prerequisites/small blocks; discrimination after component introduction; and separate explanation, performance and delayed retention. Methodology research adds no external course content. This was a focused evidence review, not a systematic review or claim of one universally optimal learning plan.

Recall uses `uni-fixed-v1`: 1 day after supported acquisition, then 6 after the first eligible delayed pass, then half-up-rounded previous interval ×2.5 with at least one-day growth. These are transparent defaults. The [published SM-2 algorithm](https://super-memory.org/archive/english/ol/sm2.htm) additionally changes item-specific ease from 0–5 ratings and uses ceiling/repetition rules; this implementation does not claim to be complete SM-2. The default 24-hour delayed criterion is also a pragmatic guard, not a fitted personal optimum.

Actual offset-aware response timestamps become Europe/Prague dates; date arithmetic handles month/year/leap/DST boundaries. Calendar next-day tasks can be due before 24 elapsed hours: early/unknown-exposure passes cannot extend the delayed ladder. Wrong/partial/assisted delayed answers open a one-day corrective/reacquisition gate. No response preserves overdue dates. A qualifying overdue answer anchors the next interval to the actual answer date. Explicit scheduling changes remain scheduling decisions and preserve the original due history.

## How to invoke

- `$ingest <exact material folder>`: ingest and independently verify sources/notes, then hand completed scope to plan.
- `$uni-teach:plan Prepare <full course path>, <bounded topic>`: create/reconcile complete lesson contracts from finished notes.
- `$teach Teach/resume/review <course/topic>; I have <budget>` or `$uni-teach:teach`: conduct the prepared session and commit actual observations.
- `$uni-teach:recall Synchronize committed reviews with Todoist`: calculate and synchronize reviews in verified existing projects; no qualifying evidence means no task.

Select a capable model for ingest/plan and a faster model for prepared teach/recall. The skill cannot switch the host model. Missing academic completion goes to ingest; missing teaching/assessment design goes to plan; due reviews go to teach; only committed learner results go to recall.
