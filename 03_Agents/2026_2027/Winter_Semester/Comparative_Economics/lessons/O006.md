## Motivation, intuition, and definitions

Useful institutional function does not establish the cause of origin: Roland calls that inference the functionalist fallacy. Inefficiency need not disappear because reform requires organized costly action and may threaten beneficiaries. Collective-action/free-riding, inhibited association and coordination on low trust can obstruct change. Path dependence means earlier arrangements shape later resources, power and expectations, not that history is immutable. Survivorship bias arises when surviving traditional societies are treated as the original form of all such societies. The lecture’s feedback model distinguishes de jure legally assigned power from de facto effective power rooted partly in resources. The Narrow Corridor uses state power vertical and society power horizontal; it is qualitative and belongs to the lecture, not the uploaded Why Nations Fail chapter.

## Explanation steps

1. Explain what a rule does now, then ask a distinct historical question about why it arose. The ability to lower transaction costs is neither proof of intentional efficient design nor proof it will appear automatically.
2. Identify who would gain from reform, who bears participation/switching costs and who loses privilege. A low-trust pattern can be individually stable even with a better joint pattern; beneficiaries may impede organization.
3. Read feedback in time order: today’s political institutions shape de jure power; resource distribution shapes de facto power; both shape today’s economic institutions and tomorrow’s political institutions. Economic institutions affect performance and next resource distribution, which influences later effective power. Arrows are proposed mechanisms/endogeneity, not coefficients or identities.
4. Read corridor qualitatively: constrained state capacity with organized society is Shackled Leviathan; dominant state is Despotic; weak state is Absent; weak-both is Paper. These illustrative labels/country placements have no measured coordinate or inevitable country route.
5. Explain why transition may be imposed or have no clear start decision/date, why China/Russia examples differ, and why political control can sustain an economically poor arrangement. Earlier arrangements constrain options but reform/conflict remains possible. Thinkers Veblen/Commons/Coase/Williamson/North/Olson are conceptual connections in Roland, not six independent empirical tests. No scholar-name recall criterion is added.
