## Motivation and explanation

EUR is the quantity delivered and USD/EUR is its unit price. Thus both invoice and futures gain are in USD. Let X=N×25000 EUR. A matched physical purchase costs X×F_T USD. The cumulative long gain is X(F_T−F_0); subtracting it from the invoice leaves X×F_0. Dividing by X gives the initial USD/EUR quote. This algebra uses the same delivered quantity and endpoint and excludes funding, fees and mismatch. It does not imply the account needs no interim collateral. Closing earlier instead creates X(F_close−F_0) and ends the contract: the final delivery branch is an alternative. Benchmark sampling/trim rules define EDSP; a reported quote is not automatically that benchmark.

## Misconceptions and correction

An invoice plus a favourable long gain usually signals confusion between cash received and purchase cost. Write invoice as an outflow, gain as an inflow; subtract once, using matching EUR amounts. Observe the learner's reasoning before diagnosing: a sign or omitted word alone is tentative. Score before help.
