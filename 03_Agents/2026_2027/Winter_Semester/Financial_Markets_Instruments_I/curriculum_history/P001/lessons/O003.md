## Outcome and conditions

For a fixed position, produce daily settlement P/L, pre-call balance, call/top-up and ending account balance, then reconcile final balance to deposits/withdrawals and endpoint P/L. State the strict-below threshold, chosen restoration target and withdrawal policy before computing. Distinguish both source uses of variation margin. No settlement-flow interest is modeled.

## Motivation, intuition, and definitions

Motivation: A profitable final trade can still demand cash on earlier days. I is initial deposit, M maintenance minimum, R stated restoration target, G daily signed P/L, A^- pre-adjustment balance, C top-up and W withdrawal. A^-=previous A+G; if A^-<M, C=R−A^-; otherwise C=0; A=A^-+C−W. Equality with M is no call under this strict rule. Both sides deposit initial margin. Leverage means a full-contract movement can be large relative to initial deposit. The source's 5–15% illustration is not a universal requirement. Variation margin can name daily settlement transfers or additional deposits; record G and C separately.

## Explanation steps

1. Use adjacent settlement prices to compute G_long=Nq(F_t−F_previous), G_short opposite. Fixed N/q is required; changing positions belongs to O005.
2. Add G to previous ending account before funding. Compare this A^- with M.
3. Only after a breach compute R−A^-. Initial and maintenance restoration produce different balances and deposits but identical trading result. Do not silently choose a target when none is given.
4. Withdraw only under stated retained-balance policy. A deposit is cash funding; a withdrawal is cash removal; neither is a trade gain/loss.
5. Sum G: middle prices cancel, giving Nq(F_final−F_open) for a long. Reconcile P/L=A_final+total withdrawals−total deposits (including initial).
6. A missed call can trigger forced reverse trade at trader cost. Funding along the path remains necessary even when final P/L is favorable. This is why a final payoff calculation alone is not a liquidity plan.

## Misconceptions and correction

Signs: computing cumulative change anew every day; calling at equality; using M as the deposit; calling final balance profit. Correction: adjacent-price transfer first, then pre-call balance, threshold, chosen target. Hint1: identify A^- before any deposit. Hint2: C=R−A^- only after breach. O003-C1 / v1: Previous account90, long daily loss40, M60,R90. Key A^-=50,C40,A90; P/L for this day−40, deposit40 is funding.
