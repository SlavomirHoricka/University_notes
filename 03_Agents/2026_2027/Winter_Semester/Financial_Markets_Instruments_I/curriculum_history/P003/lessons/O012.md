## Motivation and explanation

Bond quotes are prices per100 face. A100000-face contract therefore multiplies each quote point by1000. Clean price omits accrued coupon; full price adds it. A conversion factor prices the deliverable at the contract reference yield, rather than its market yield: PV the remaining coupons and principal at the later coupon date, add the coupon due at that date and discount the combined cash value back to the earlier valuation date using the declared fraction, subtract reference accrued coupon, divide by100. The finished-note example uses semiannual coupon4 and periodic reference yield.035; its later-date value P_AN=Σ(j=1..22)4/(1.035)^j+100/(1.035)^22. Earlier full value is P_F=(P_AN+4)/(1.035)^(fraction), followed by the stated accrued-coupon subtraction and /100. Its170/171 convention conflict prevents blindly copying a numerical factor. In prepared calculations CF is declared consistently. Delivery cash per100 is futures clean price×CF+delivery accrued coupon. A short carry trade borrows, buys the cash bond, receives/reinvests coupons, delivers and repays. Simplified daily carry = annual coupon/365−full purchase price×funding rate/365. Positive constant carry rewards later delivery, negative earlier, only under the note's simplified conditions. CF corrects reference characteristics, not actual funding economics; CTD needs eligible-basket comparison.

## Misconceptions and correction

Observe the learner's reasoning before diagnosing: a sign or omitted word alone is tentative. Score before help.
