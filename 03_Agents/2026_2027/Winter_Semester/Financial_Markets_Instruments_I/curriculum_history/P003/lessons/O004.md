## Outcome and conditions

Given a long/short, quote units, exposure, initial/maintenance balances and withdrawal rule, derive the strict margin-call price inequality, test equality and derive a withdrawal price condition for retaining initial or maintenance. Use a single uninterrupted price move before deposits/withdrawals; no tick grid is imposed unless stated.

## Motivation, intuition, and definitions

Motivation: What price first creates a cash call, and what price supports an intended withdrawal? Let X=Nq total exposure. Long balance A=I+X(F−F0); short A=I+X(F0−F). Use F in dollars if X is physical units. A call needs A<M; equality is not a breach. To withdraw W while retaining K, require A−W≥K, hence A≥K+W. K is a policy input; choosing initial versus maintenance changes this condition.

## Explanation steps

1. Put the quote in consistent units and write the signed balance before any intervening cash adjustment.
2. Substitute the strict-below criterion: long F<F0−(I−M)/X; short F>F0+(I−M)/X. Derive rather than memorize the adverse direction.
3. At equality the loss exactly consumes the I−M cushion, so A=M and there is no call under this model. If a contract tick grid were supplied, the first allowed price strictly beyond threshold would matter; no grid is invented.
4. Withdrawal needs A≥K+W. Long requires F≥F0+(K+W−I)/X; short F≤F0−(K+W−I)/X. A withdrawal can be possible immediately if initial cash already exceeds K+W.
5. A target for a call and K for withdrawal are distinct rules. Source exercises do not set one universal withdrawal policy, so report conditional results and the exact assumptions.

## Misconceptions and correction

Signs: adverse direction reversed; call at equality; same withdrawal answer regardless K. Correction: write A−W≥K and calculate the balance at the claimed price. Hint1: ask which side loses on a rise. Hint2: compare A with M, not I. O004-C1 / v1: Short X1000,F0=2,I500,M300. Is F2.20 a call? At F2.00 can W100 be withdrawn retaining M? Key equality A300 no call; at opening A500−100=400≥300 yes. Pass both with balance; partial unsupported conclusion; fail equality call/retained-rule confusion.

The sign alone is a tentative diagnosis; use the learner's reasoning to establish the confusion. Score the initial response before any hint. Hint 1 points to the named distinction; hint 2 points to the first correct step. After two ineffective hints show the prepared key, then pause if the fresh C1 response still fails.
