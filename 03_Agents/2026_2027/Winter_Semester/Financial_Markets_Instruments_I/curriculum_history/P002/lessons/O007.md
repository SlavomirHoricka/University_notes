## Motivation, intuition, and definitions

Motivation: A losing futures leg can be part of a successful price hedge. X=Nq matched units; F0 opening, Ft closing, St spot at hedge transaction date; B=St−Ft. A buyer opens long because a rise in later purchase cost should be offset by a futures gain. A seller opens short because a fall in sale receipt should be offset by a gain. Define E positive expenditure, CF buyer signed cash flow with outflows negative, and R seller positive net receipt.

## Explanation steps

1. Buyer: futures gain X(Ft−F0); spot purchase costs XSt. E=XSt−X(Ft−F0)=X[F0+(St−Ft)]=X(F0+B). CF=−E.
2. Seller: spot revenue XSt plus short gain X(F0−Ft). R=XSt+X(F0−Ft)=X(F0+B). The same expression has a different economic label; do not swap buyer expenditure and seller inflow signs.
3. At matching maturity conditional convergence B_T0 gives E=R=XF0 and CF=−XF0. Futures loss on a falling market is offset by cheaper purchase; futures loss on rising market by higher sale price for seller.
4. Before maturity the closing basis is unknown at inception. The early hedge still operates but leaves E/R=XF0+XB_t rather than a guaranteed opening-price lock. This is basis risk, not the same as absolute spot movement.
5. The notes flag the source verbal early-hedge sign error: −F0+(Ft−St)=−F0−B, buyer signed flow. Positive expenditure is F0+B. Never reuse the mistaken label.
6. Zero-basis effective-price accounting does not remove interim calls. Daily settlement requires funding and the source ignores its interest; a final hedge outcome is not proof of no cash demand.

## Misconceptions and correction

Signs: a losing futures leg treated as failed hedge; wrong hedge direction; buyer cost subtracted with wrong sign; early basis assumed zero. Correction: write two separate economic legs before combining; distinguish E from CF. Hint1: buyer needs gains when purchase price rises. Hint2: E=spot cost−long gain. O007-C1 / v1: X10,F0=10,Ft9,St8. Give buyer E/CF and interpret. Key long−10,spot80,E90,CF−90,B−1,effective9; loss on leg is offset by cheaper spot, early remaining basis.
