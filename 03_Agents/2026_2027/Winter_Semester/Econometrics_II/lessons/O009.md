## Motivation and explanation

Dates encode order and lags; they are not interchangeable sampled labels. The whole design X contains every sample-date regressor and an intercept. Rank k+1 makes the OLS inverse meaningful. Strict exogeneity conditions the date-t error mean on all of X, including future values. Same-date E[u_t|x_t]=0 omits potential feedback: an unexpected outcome today may alter policy/resources tomorrow. Conditional on X, the OLS weights (X′X)^−1X′ are fixed; therefore E[βhat|X]=β+A E[u|X]=β when the strict-exogeneity condition makes that full mean vector zero. Then iterate expectation for unconditional unbiasedness. Constant variance, serial covariance and normality enter different results later, not this cancellation. Zero conditional mean implies appropriate zero covariances with finite moments but the converse is not automatic. Failure removes a guarantee, not a proof of universal bias. Suitable weaker conditions may give consistency in contextual Chapter11 passages, without finite-sample unbiasedness; a full next-week theorem is outside these notes. Names/formulas avoid the deck/textbook rank/exogeneity numbering swap.
