03

Key definitions and concepts

  • Carbon-to-CO2 conversion: Mass multiplication by44/12 for the same carbon amount.
  • CO2-equivalent emissions: Mass weighted by the declared gas metric and time horizon.
  • Atmospheric stock: The accumulated amount after additions and removals over compatible intervals.
04

Carbon units, stocks, flows and emissions charts

Which quantities can be compared when a chart says carbon, CO2, CO2-equivalent, concentration or per person? The optional-reading section supplies unit conversions and charts.

Reading their units and boundaries is a foundation for both climate-policy comparisons and monetary costs per tonne. These pages are included because they are inside the selected supplied document, while retaining their “Voluntary readings” status.

The map follows pp. 47–58 and uses p. 26's GWP definition to separate mass conversion from warming equivalence.

4.1 Carbon mass and the inverse price conversion

A tonne of carbon (t C) measures carbon atoms; a tonne of CO2 measures the whole molecule. Using source atomic masses 12 for C and 16 for O, molecular mass is \(12+2(16)=44\). Therefore

\[1\ \mathrm{tCO_2}=\frac{12}{44}\ \mathrm{tC}\approx0.2727\ \mathrm{tC},\qquad 1\ \mathrm{tC}=\frac{44}{12}\ \mathrm{tCO_2}\approx3.667\ \mathrm{tCO_2}.\]

For the same total payment, multiplying mass by \(44/12\) requires dividing price per mass by \(44/12\). Thus

\[100\ \$/\mathrm{tC}\times\frac{12}{44}=27.27\ \$/\mathrm{tCO_2},\qquad 100\ \$/\mathrm{tCO_2}\times\frac{44}{12}=366.67\ \$/\mathrm{tC}.\]

Confusing quantity conversion with price conversion reverses the comparison. Neither operation is a GWP conversion for methane or other gases.

4.2 GWP and CO2-equivalent emissions

Global warming potential (GWP) compares the time-integrated radiative effect of a pulse mass of gas with an equal mass of CO2 over a stated horizon; CO2's value is one by definition. For a chosen metric, horizon and assessment,

\[E_{CO_2e}=\sum_g m_g GWP_g,\]

where \(m_g\) is emitted mass of gas \(g\). This is Explanation for the source's examples, not a new emissions estimate. A million tonnes of \(\mathrm{CH}_4\) at \(\mathrm{GWP}_{100}=25\) becomes \(25\) million tonnes \(\mathrm{CO}_2\mathrm{e}\); a million tonnes of \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}\) at \(298\) becomes \(298\) million tonnes \(\mathrm{CO}_2\mathrm{e}\).

4.4 Why a stable flow can increase an atmospheric stock

Concentration measures an atmospheric stock; emissions add mass per unit time and sinks remove it. A simple Explanation of this accounting is

\[A_{t+1}-A_t=E_t-R_t,\]

where \(A_t\) is atmospheric mass, \(E_t\) emissions and \(R_t\) net removal over the same interval, all in compatible mass units. A constant positive emission flow still increases \(A\) whenever \(E_t>R_t\). Stabilizing emissions differs from bringing net additions to zero. Connecting mass to ppm requires an atmospheric-mass conversion, which the source does not supply.

uses separate axes for concentration (ppm) and annual emissions (gigatonnes). Its 1751–2022 graph and caption's historical increase from about 5 Gt/year in the mid twentieth century to over 35 Gt/year illustrate different quantities, not a fitted causal coefficient. draws a flat emission line and rising concentration curve on uncalibrated x1–x20 axes with no dates, units or source.

Worked example

Prompt: Use a declared hypothetical inventory with \(12\ \mathrm{tC}\) and price \(p_C=100\ \mathrm{currency\ units/tC}\). Convert mass and the price for the same total payment into tCO2 and price/tCO2.

For separate emissions of 1 t CH4 and 0.5 t N2O, use the explicitly historical AR4 GWP100 factors 25 and 298 to compute tCO2e and distinguish it from CO2-equivalent atmospheric concentration/AGGI. With atmospheric mass \(A_t=100\), emissions \(10\) and removals \(6\) in common mass units over one interval, compute \(A_{t+1}\) and explain stable-flow versus stable-stock claims and missing ppm conversion.

Country A has 5 t CO2e/person and population 2 million, countryB 10 t CO2e/person and 0.5 million: compute totals and interpret rectangle height/width/area. State the necessary chart year/gas/horizon/boundary/log-axis/missing-data cautions and optional-reading status.

Solution: Mass scales as \(m_{CO_2}=m_C(44/12)=44.00000000\ \mathrm{tCO_2}\), while the equal-payment unit price scales inversely, \(p_{CO_2}=p_C(12/44)=27.27272727\) currency units/tCO2. Both products give the same total \(1200.00000000\) currency units.

This molecular-mass conversion is not methane GWP. Historical AR4 GWP100 gives \(E_{CO_2e}=1(25)+0.5(298)=174.00000000\) t CO2e;

horizon/assessment must be declared, not treated as current universal constants. A forcing-equivalent atmospheric mixing ratio is another quantity;

AGGI is an index of long-lived-gas forcing normalized to 1 in the base year 1990, not emitted tonnes. Stock accounting gives \(A_{t+1}=A_t+E_t-R_t=104.00000000\);

a stable positive gross flow grows the stock whenever emissions exceed removals. Constant net additions imply a linear stock increase, not the source’s uncalibrated curved law;

mass-to-ppm conversion is absent. Country totals are \(10.00000000\) and \(5.00000000\) million tonnes: height is per person, width population, area total;

a higher per-person value need not imply larger total. Exact comparisons require common year/source/gas boundaries (CO2 versus all-gas CO2e, land-use inclusion), metric horizon and vintage.

The source title/axis CO2/CO2e conflict is unresolved; negative net land-use is not missing/hatched data.

6.4 Another chance to practise

Mass in t CO2 is 44/12 times mass in t C.

Solve the following:

  1. To preserve the same payment, does price per t CO2 also multiply by 44/12?

A local draft for your own study.

Reveal explanation

No. It divides by 44/12, or multiplies by 12/44; quantity times unit price must remain the same. This is molecular mass accounting, not another gas’s GWP.

Check your reasoning: an offline quiz

Choose one answer per question. Check individual answers or check the whole quiz. Each option includes feedback on the reasoning, and everything works offline.

7.1 Question 1

12 tonnes carbon are priced at 100 currency units/tC. Which CO 2 mass and equivalent unit price preserve payment?

Reveal explanation

Mass multiplies by 44/12; price multiplies by 12/44; total payment stays 1200.

7.2 Question 2

Stock 100, interval emissions 10 and removals 6 in common mass units. What is next stock?

Reveal explanation

Add emissions and subtract removals; constant positive gross flow does not imply constant stock.

7.3 Question 3

A has 5 tonnes/person and 2 million people; B has 10 and 0.5 million. What are total emissions?

Reveal explanation

Rectangle height times population width gives total area, using a common gas/year boundary.
